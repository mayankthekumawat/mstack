# AI Cell report format

This profile follows the September 2026 personal MPR supplied as a reference. It describes the reusable format and writing preferences. The report and its private evidence stay outside this public skill.

- Title: `Monthly progress report | AI Cell`.
- Identity: name, designation, reporting month and year.
- `1. Project-wise progress`, grouped by project. Label delivered work separately from work tested or prepared during the month.
- Within each project, retain useful subsections such as `Other updates and impact`, `Next month`, and `Blockers and unresolved feedback` when relevant.
- `2. Miscellaneous work done`.
- `3. Domains / new areas being worked on`.
- `4. Overall remarks`, containing `-` unless the user changes that preference.
- An optional evidence appendix for supported quantitative claims. A cost appendix from an earlier report does not require a new appendix with unverified numbers.

Write for a GM at NHAI. Explain what officers can do, which service problem changed, and the remaining operational limits. Use natural prose covering the issue, change, and benefit. The original author explicitly rejected headings that spelled out those three parts and found terse achievement lists insufficient.

Use A4 portrait, about 51 pt side margins, Arial or a comparable sans serif, a 20 pt title, an approximately 10.2 pt body with 13.6 pt leading, and restrained dark blue headings. Continued pages can have a shorter title. Put the author's name and reporting period at the lower left and `page / total` at the lower right. Match the reference's readable spacing; do not force the same page count when content differs.

Do not copy September's achievements, message milestone, cost comparison, or next-month plans into another month as facts. Recheck each carried-forward item. A later production launch cannot be claimed as an October delivery merely because its staging work occurred in October.
